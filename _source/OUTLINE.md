## The client's requirements (Yousif, in the client's own words)

- **Skills:** What can we do with them, and how do we use them?
- **Connectors and Plugins:** How do we use connectors and integrate plugins?
- **AI Employees:** We want to understand how to create AI employees that can perform our work in different roles,
  such as a producer, marketer, designer, or accountant. How can we create and use them?
- **Cowork and Artifacts:** We want to understand the fundamentals of these features, how to use them, and the purpose of each feature.
- Essentially, they want to learn the fundamentals of Claude and leave the workshop with a strong foundation that allows them
  to continue exploring and learning independently.

**Participants' existing knowledge:** they already have some background knowledge of Claude ("this is not our first time using it").
They want a strong foundation, to understand the available features and their purposes, and to be confident enough to build on it independently.
