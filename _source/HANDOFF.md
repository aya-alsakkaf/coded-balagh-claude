**What is different from AI Essentials (the reason for this session):** no prompting lesson. Every block is a Claude feature, shown in a
slide demo, then done hands-on. The roadmap (the four layers) is on screen in the first 25 minutes.

## 2 · Outcomes

1. I can choose between Claude's trained knowledge and web search, and give Claude PDFs, spreadsheets and screenshots to work from.
2. I can turn a spreadsheet or an idea into an artifact, such as a dashboard, a calculator or a short deck, and share it.
3. I can connect my email and calendar to Claude and keep every send and change under my approval.
4. I can teach Claude once with memory, instructions, a Project and a Skill, and find ready-made skills and plugins.
5. I can explain what Cowork and scheduled tasks do, and set up an AI employee for one role at my organization.

## 3 · The day

**Claim:** Claude is more than a chat box. Each layer you switch on makes it do more of the work, and you stay the manager.
**Integration moment:** one AI employee for one Misbah role (producer, marketer, designer or accountant), built from the layers of the morning.

## 6 · Assessment

MAP test: one sitting at 09:05 (PRE), the same 12 questions at 12:40 (POST). 10 minutes each, not graded, no "I don't know", phone only.
Part 1: the 5 outcomes as confidence items (1 to 5). Part 2: 12 questions, mostly work moments at Misbah (judgment, not recall).

1. Fahad asks Claude for today's USD to KWD rate. Web search is off. What is Claude's answer based on?
   **a) What Claude learned in training, which may be out of date** · b) The live rate, because Claude checks it each morning · c) The rate on the Central Bank of Kuwait site today · d) An error, because Claude cannot work with currencies
2. Hessa needs total donations by campaign from a 240-row Excel file. Which request gives the most reliable numbers?
   a) Paste the first 50 rows into the chat and ask · b) Send a screenshot of the sheet and ask for totals · **c) Attach the file and ask Claude to calculate with code** · d) Describe the file in words and ask for an estimate
3. Noura wants a winter-kit cost calculator that volunteers can open again next week. What should she ask Claude for?
   a) A long answer that explains the formula · **b) An artifact: a small calculator she can share** · c) A memory entry with the unit costs · d) A scheduled task that sends the costs daily
4. Claude's dashboard shows Winter Warmth KWD 2,500 above the Control totals sheet. What do you do first?
   a) Share it, because Claude used code to calculate · b) Ask Claude to round the numbers before you share · c) Remove the Winter Warmth chart and share the rest · **d) Look for a duplicate row behind that total**
5. Claude drafts a reply to a supplier through the Gmail connector. You work in Manual mode. What happens before the email goes out?
   a) Nothing: Claude sends it as soon as it is ready · b) Gmail holds it for one hour, then sends it · **c) Claude asks you to allow or deny the send** · d) Your manager gets a copy to approve first
6. Which item is red on the safe-use card, so it stays out of Claude?
   a) Rewriting a public post about the winter campaign · **b) The donor list with names, phone numbers and amounts** · c) Practice with the fictional Misbah sample files · d) A summary of a public news article about winter
7. Every winter-campaign chat needs the brand guide, but your other work does not. Where do you put the brand guide?
   **a) In a Project's knowledge, with the campaign rules** · b) In Instructions for Claude, under Settings · c) In Claude's memory, by saying it once in a chat · d) At the start of every new chat, pasted by hand
8. Fahad writes donor thank-you letters every week with the same five steps. What makes Claude follow those steps when the task comes up?
   **a) A Skill with the steps, an example and a template** · b) A longer prompt saved in a file on his desktop · c) A connector to the shared drive with old letters · d) A Project with no files and no instructions
9. What does a plugin add to Claude?
   a) A faster model for long tasks · b) Extra storage space for your files · c) A separate chat window for each colleague · **d) A bundle of skills and connectors for a role**
10. Which task fits Cowork better than a normal chat?
    a) Explaining the meaning of one word in a post · **b) Sorting 14 receipt files into one Excel table** · c) Checking the date in one short email · d) Translating one sentence of a post into Arabic
11. You want a donations summary every Sunday at 08:00, even when your laptop is off. What do you set up?
    a) A memory entry that says "every Sunday" · b) A Skill named weekly summary · c) A pinned chat you open each Sunday · **d) A scheduled task in Claude**
12. You set up an AI accountant for Misbah. Which part should stay with a person?
    a) Reading the receipts in the folder · b) Sorting the donations by campaign · **c) Approving any payment or email that goes out** · d) Drafting the weekly three-line note
