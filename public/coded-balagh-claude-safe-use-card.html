Safe-use card

Before you connect an app or paste anything, sort the task. Your organization's AI and data policy always comes first.

Green, amber, red Three risks Policy first

Green: go ahead

Public or fictional data, and your own drafting.

  • Rewrite a public campaign post
  • Practice on the Misbah files
  • Explain a Claude feature

Amber: approved account, approvals on

Internal work only in an account your organization approved. Remove names first. Keep approvals on.

  • Search your work inbox for supplier emails
  • Totals by campaign with donor IDs, no names
  • Replies saved as drafts

Red: never in an AI tool

Personal data of donors or families, case files, civil IDs, bank details. Any send or payment without a person.

  • The donor list with phone numbers
  • A family's case file
  • Civil IDs or bank details
  • Emails sent to donors with no approval
Three risks, three habits
RiskWhat it looks likeThe habit
Wrong factsClaude predicts words. A neat answer can be wrong.Check every number, date and name against the source.
Data leavingA personal account is not your organization's approved tool.Sort the task first: green, amber or red. Follow your policy.
Actions without youConnectors and Cowork can send, create and delete.Work in Manual mode. Approve each send, event and file change.
Your organization's policy comes first
  • Ask your General Manager or IT which AI accounts and connectors your organization approves.
  • If there is no approved account for work data, amber becomes red.
  • Donor and family data stays red, in any account.
  • When you are not sure, treat the task as red and ask first.
