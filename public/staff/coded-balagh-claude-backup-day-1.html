Layer 3 · Skills · Demo 3.2

Create the donor thank-you skill with Claude

Claude · Skills

Inside donor-thank-you/SKILL.md

PartWhat it says
namedonor-thank-you
descriptionWrite a thank-you letter to a Misbah donor. Use when someone asks for a donor thank-you or receipt letter.
stepsGreet the donor by name, or “Dear supporter” if there is no name. · Name the campaign and the amount in KWD with three decimals. · Say what the gift paid for (one winter kit = KWD 46.500). · Add one warm line of thanks. · End with the receipt number and the Misbah sign-off.
exampleOne good letter, under 120 words
