Claude in Layers · CODED · Thursday 26 November 2026 · 09:00-13:00 · CODED campus, Legacy room. CODED Professionals client session for Al-Balagh Al-Mubeen Charity. Al-Balagh Al-Mubeen staff who already use Claude, including the General Manager; they know prompting and asked for Claude's features in depth. No prompting lesson. Story: Misbah Charity Society (fictional). Tools: Claude: chat, web search, files, Cowork, artifacts, connectors, memory, Projects, Skills and scheduled tasks. Habit: Claude drafts. You check. You approve what goes out.
Outcomes
  1. I can choose between Claude's trained knowledge and web search, and give Claude PDFs, spreadsheets and screenshots to work from.
  2. I can turn a spreadsheet or an idea into an artifact, such as a dashboard, a calculator or a short deck, and share it.
  3. I can connect my email and calendar to Claude and keep every send and change under my approval.
  4. I can teach Claude once with memory, instructions, a Project and a Skill, and find ready-made skills.
  5. I can explain what Cowork and scheduled tasks do, and set up an AI employee for one role at my organization.
