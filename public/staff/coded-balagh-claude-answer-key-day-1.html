Task 3.2

A working skill and one tested letter with all five parts.
Task 3.2 · 12 min · on your own

Install the starter skill, test it, make it yours

misbah-donor-thanks-skill.zip
Hint (the participant pages show this)The receipt number is the step people forget. Check it first.
Answer

The five parts to check

  1. Greet the donor by name, or “Dear supporter” if there is no name.
  2. Name the campaign and the amount in KWD with three decimals.
  3. Say what the gift paid for (one winter kit = KWD 46.500).
  4. Add one warm line of thanks.
  5. End with the receipt number and the Misbah sign-off.
KWD 150.000 ÷ KWD 46.500 is 3.2 kits, so “about three winter kits” is right.
