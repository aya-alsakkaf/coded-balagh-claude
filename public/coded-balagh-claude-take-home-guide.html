Take-home guide

Keep going on your own, one layer at a time. Start with green tasks. Your organization's AI and data policy comes first.

Your own work, safely First week, first month Policy first
01

Your first week

  1. Build one artifact you will use again: a calculator, a dashboard or a short deck. Check one number before you share it.
  2. Connect Gmail and Google Calendar only in an account your organization approved for Claude. Ask for Sunday's brief, read only. Keep every send as a draft.
  3. Create one Project for your main area of work, with its rules and two files.
02

Your first month

  1. Turn one method you repeat every week into a Skill. Test it, then share it: from the skill on Team and Enterprise plans, or send the .zip.
  2. Schedule one weekly task you have already run and checked by hand.
  3. Hire one AI employee per role: producer, marketer, designer, accountant. Each one drafts; a person approves.
03

Keep exploring

  1. The Claude Help Center (support.claude.com) explains every feature, step by step.
  2. Claude Academy (academy.claude.com) has free short courses, including Introduction to Claude Cowork.
  3. The Plugins page in the sidebar lists ready-made skills and connectors. Search there before you build.
  4. Claude Code is the next layer: Claude builds tools and small apps with you.
!The habit · every time. Claude drafts. You check. You approve what goes out. Your organization's AI and data policy comes first.
