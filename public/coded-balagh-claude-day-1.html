You build today: One AI employee for one Misbah role, built from every layer of the morning.

Your turn

7 hands-on tasks. The timer runs on the main screen. Every prompt is on your prompt sheet.

Your turn · Task 1.1 · 15 min · on your own

One chat, the web and three files

  1. Run prompt 1 (older layout: turn on Web search in the + menu first). Open the link it gives.
  2. Attach the report (PDF), the donations file (Excel) and the post (PNG). Run prompts 2 to 4.
  3. Check the page Claude gives for each error in the post.
15:00 on the clock · Claude · web search and files
You hand in: The KWD amount with the rate's date and link, and the 2 errors in the post with their pages.
Look for: A rate with no date or link came from training. The post has 2 errors: a date and a number.
Finished early? Open the Claude app on your phone. Ask the PDF question by voice: tap the voice mode icon (the sound wave), not the microphone.

Your turn · Task 1.3 · 15 min · on your own

Build one artifact for Misbah

  1. Choose A to E. Attach its file and paste its prompt.
  2. Ask for one change. Then check one number against the file.
  3. Open Share and copy the link (private is fine).
15:00 on the clock · Claude · artifacts
You hand in: One working artifact, one number checked against its file, and its share link.
Look for: A total that is too high often hides a row counted twice.
Finished early? Build a second artifact from another choice, or add an “Ask Claude” box inside your artifact.

Your turn · Task 2.1 · 15 min · on your own

Connect your email and calendar, then get Sunday's brief

  1. Connect only an account your organization approved for Claude. No approval, or donor or family emails in it? Attach misbah-inbox-and-calendar.pdf instead.
  2. Approved: + › Connectors, turn on Gmail and Calendar, sign in. Set the mode to Manual. Run the brief for your next working day, read only.
  3. Ask for one draft reply. Do not send it.
  4. Check two facts in the brief against the email or the calendar itself.
15:00 on the clock · Claude · Gmail and Google Calendar
You hand in: Your brief table, one draft reply (not sent), and two facts checked against the source.
Look for: The urgent email changes a date or a number.
Finished early? Ask Claude to find a free 30-minute slot next week for the Ramadan Iftar 2027 planning meeting. Approve the event only if it is real.
misbah-inbox-and-calendar.pdf
Your turn · Task 3.1 · 8 min · on your own

Set your instructions and build the campaign Project

  1. Settings › Your Claude › Instructions (older layout: Settings › Account): add two lines about how you like answers.
  2. Create a Project named Misbah Winter Campaign. Paste its instructions from your prompt sheet.
  3. Add the brand guide and the brief to the Project knowledge.
  4. Start a chat in the Project and run the request. Check one caption against the brand guide.
8:00 on the clock · Claude · Projects
You hand in: A Project with instructions and two files, and one caption checked against the brand guide.
Look for: One number each, checked against the brief. English, then Arabic. The link comes last.
Finished early? Open Settings › Your Claude (older layout: Settings › Memory). Read what Claude remembers about you. Edit or delete one item.
misbah-brand-guide.docxmisbah-campaign-brief.docx
Your turn · Task 3.2 · 12 min · on your own

Install the starter skill, test it, make it yours

  1. Upload the .zip (do not unzip it). Older layout: Customize › Skills › + › Create skill › Upload a skill. Plugins page: Add › Upload.
  2. Start a new chat. Type / and choose donor-thank-you, then paste the test request.
  3. Check the letter: all five parts, under 120 words.
  4. Make it yours: ask Claude to change one step, then test again.
12:00 on the clock · Claude · Skills
You hand in: A working skill and one tested letter with all five parts.
Look for: The receipt number is the step people forget. Check it first.
Finished early? Open the Plugins page (Customize on some accounts), filter by skills and add one skill you would use at work.
misbah-donor-thanks-skill.zip
Your turn · Task 4.1 · 8 min · on your own

Hand Cowork a folder

  1. Unzip misbah-receipts-nov-2026.zip on your laptop.
  2. Start a Cowork task in the Claude desktop app and add the folder. Choose Manual mode.
  3. Paste the task. Approve each step you agree with.
  4. Open the Excel file. Check the two flags against the receipts.
8:00 on the clock · Claude · Cowork
You hand in: The Excel table and the 2 flags, checked against the receipts.
Look for: One receipt has no campaign code. One receipt is in the folder twice.
Finished early? In an account your organization approved, put the unzipped folder on Google Drive and turn on Google Drive in + › Connectors. Then schedule the job for every Thursday at 08:00: Scheduled › New task, or ask in the chat. Or add the Finance plugin.
misbah-receipts-nov-2026.zip
Your turn · Task 4.2 · 20 min · on your own

Hire one AI employee

  1. Choose a role. Create a Project named for it, for example Misbah AI Accountant.
  2. Paste its job description (Demo 4.2 on your prompt sheet) and add its files.
  3. Add the role's plugin from the Plugins page. Run its first job from your prompt sheet.
  4. Check one fact. Write the employee card: role, tools, schedule, approvals.
20:00 on the clock · Claude · Projects, skills, plugins
You hand in: A working AI employee Project, its first output checked, and its employee card.
Look for: The job description must end with the approval rule.
Finished early? Add the role's weekly job as a scheduled task (Scheduled › New task, or ask in the chat), set to wait for your approval.

What you will be able to do

  • Choose between trained knowledge and web search, and give Claude files to work from.
  • Turn a spreadsheet or an idea into an artifact you can share.
  • Connect email and calendar, with every send under your approval.
  • Teach Claude once with memory, instructions, a Project and a Skill, and find ready-made skills and plugins.
  • Explain Cowork and scheduled tasks, and set up one AI employee for a role.
